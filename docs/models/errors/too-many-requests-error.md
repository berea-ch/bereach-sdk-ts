# TooManyRequestsError

Read error.quotaKind. credits: nothing is left to spend; add money at error.upgradeUrl. On a plan, error.retryAfter is when the monthly allowance refills; with error.details naming the workspace day ceiling, searching resumes after error.retryAfter and money does not help. Any other kind: wait error.retryAfter seconds.
