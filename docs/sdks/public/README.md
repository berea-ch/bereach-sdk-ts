# Public

## Overview

Cookieless public data: read public profiles, posts and companies with no connected account and no session rate budget spent

### Available Operations

* [publicProfile](#publicprofile) - Read a public profile
* [publicFindPeople](#publicfindpeople) - Find people from public data
* [publicFindCompanies](#publicfindcompanies) - Find companies from public data
* [publicFindEmployees](#publicfindemployees) - Find the people at a company from public profiles
* [publicCompany](#publiccompany) - Read a public company page
* [publicFindPosts](#publicfindposts) - Find public posts by topic or by who posted
* [publicEnrich](#publicenrich) - Publicly re-fetch a list of contacts
* [planSearches](#plansearches) - Turn any material into a plan of searches
* [runSearches](#runsearches) - Run a plan of searches

## publicProfile

Read a public professional profile and save it as an enriched contact. Runs on public data only, so no connected account is needed and none of an account's action budget is spent. This is the default way to look up or enrich a person.

## publicFindPeople

Find people from public profiles: no connected account needed, never signed-in LinkedIn details, and found profiles save as contacts and render as a people list you narrate with a one-line count. One ask is one call: every plausible title, seniority or city as an array on the SAME field, any headline signal in filters.keywords, limit (1-100, default 15) set by the number or volume word they used, sentence added when they named one person rather than a crowd, and more:true, excludeWords or excludeProfiles only to continue or refine a search that already ran. When companies to leave out or a company size are set, the answer says how many people it left out: say so.

## publicFindCompanies

## planSearches

Read what someone sells and who they want to reach, from their own words, the text of documents or pages, or page addresses read by the server, and answer with a plan of people searches: what was said and what was inferred, the segments with their kinds of company, places and job titles, the searches a run would send and what they cost. While something blocking is missing the answer carries up to five questions and no searches; call again with plan_id and the answers. Free: nothing is searched, charged or saved. In a conversation the plan is that conversation's own, and a later call changes it.

## runSearches

Run a ready plan returned by plan-searches, in the background, and save the people it finds into the conversation's list, or your contacts outside one. Answers within about 25 seconds with a receipt: people added so far, credits used, and each search's state. A plan runs once: calling again with the same plan_id answers where its run stands and spends nothing more. Every search is charged to the workspace credit pool like a people search, and the run never spends more than the plan's cost_estimate_credits.
